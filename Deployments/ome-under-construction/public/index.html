Open Music Event

A community event guide for music festivals, venues, and local shows: schedules, stages, artists, maps, posters, and updates in one place.

Follow the project See latest updates

Open Music Event is

A practical event companion for when the poster is not enough.

Open Music Event is a dedicated home for your show's lineup, schedule, artists, crew, and spaces. Communities can gather and share event context; organizers get a calmer way to keep the latest event information current with their teams.

Create and manage schedules

Create and circulate performance dates, set times, stage locations, and artist details without flattening the event into a screenshot.

Put your people center stage

Artists, crews, organizers, venues, and guests can have profiles that explain why they matter to the event.

Places

Put stage locations, event maps, posters, and arrival details next to the schedule they explain.

Updates

When details change, your event guide can change too, without asking everyone to hunt through screenshots.

Credit all contributors

Credit contributors to your organization, venue, and each performance, down to hired labor and the people who make the show possible.

Open Source / Open Data

Your event information should live with you on the open internet. Calendars, schedules, artist profiles, places, and updates can flow into this app or any other.

One home for

Better event information before, during, and after the show.

For attendees

Clear schedules, stage context, contributor profiles, posters, maps, and event details in a focused app surface.

For organizers

A clearer way to keep event information organized, update it when plans change, and carry useful details forward.
